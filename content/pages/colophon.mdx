This site is built with [Next.js](https://nextjs.org), [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com), and MDX, and it's hosted on [Vercel](https://vercel.com). The source is [on GitHub](https://github.com/JustinASmith/justinsmith.sh).

## Type

Headlines are set in [Fraunces](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Fraunces), a soft, slightly wonky old-style serif. Body copy is [Geist](https://vercel.com/font), and anything that looks like it belongs in a terminal is Geist Mono.

## The pieces

- **The career trace** is hand-built: plain React and CSS, drawn to look like the trace views I spend my days in.
- **The putting green** runs on a small state machine and a timing meter. Facts you've collected are remembered in your browser, and the chains sound is synthesized on the spot.
- **The shell** opens with <kbd>/</kbd> or <kbd>⌘K</kbd>. Try `help`, `neofetch`, `putt`, or `scout`.
- **The cowbell** in the footer is synthesized in the browser with the Web Audio API, the same way a TR-808 does it.

## Colors

Paper and ink by day; a pine-dark lake by night. The accent is sunset red.
